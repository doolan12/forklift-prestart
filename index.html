Forklift Pre-Start Check

Toyota electric forklifts

Pick the model first. The checks below change to suit it.

1. Walk-around Key out, forklift off, forks lowered

2. Battery & power Electric truck checks

3. In the seat Key on, seatbelt on, in a clear area

4. Test drive & lift Slow and in a clear area, no load
